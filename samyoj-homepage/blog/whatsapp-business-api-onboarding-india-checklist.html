Prerequisites Meta checks

Before API access, Meta typically expects a legitimate business identity: Business Manager, verifiable business details, and compliance with WhatsApp Business Terms. Policy pages change—read the current version before onboarding customers in regulated industries (finance, health).

Business Manager and WABA

Create or use a Meta Business Manager account. A WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) holds phone numbers, templates, and billing. Tech providers like Samyoj connect your WABA through partner flows described in Meta’s partner documentation—customers still own the business relationship with Meta for message charges.

Phone number rules

You can migrate a number from the consumer or Business app to API, or use a new eligible number. Meta documents restrictions (VoIP limits, country support, banned histories). Plan a maintenance window: migrating may log everyone out of the old app session.

Display name and profile

Display names are reviewed against brand guidelines. Rejections delay go-live. Prepare website, billing document, or trademark proof if requested. Profile photo and category should match what customers expect when they message you.

Go-live with a tech provider

Samyoj advertises a no-code connect path for teams without developers—use the in-app wizard and support contacts on contact if Meta flags the number. First production tasks:

  1. Submit 2–3 utility templates (order update, appointment reminder).
  2. Configure a welcome message for CTWA traffic.
  3. Train agents on the 24-hour messaging window.

Takeaway: onboarding is a compliance and data project, not only a technical toggle.

Think like a marketing lead, not a software shopper

Most teams search whatsapp business api onboarding when WhatsApp already drives revenue—but ops is messy. Your buyer is usually ops managers. They do not want another login; they want fewer missed chats and a number that still works after Diwali campaigns.

Success in 30 days is simple to say and hard to do: every inbound message has an owner within business hours, promotional sends use an approved template, and you can report time-to-first-approved-template without opening five apps.

Monday to Friday rhythm

Monday: review weekend chats; assign overdue threads.
Tuesday: template performance—any spikes in blocks?
Wednesday: ad → WhatsApp handoff audit if you run Click-to-WhatsApp.
Thursday: train one new macro reply; retire one spammy broadcast.
Friday: leadership snapshot—chats opened, qualified, closed (definitions unchanged week to week).

Extra questions teams ask in week one

How long does WhatsApp API onboarding take?

Meta and your provider control the clock. A clean Standard signup can finish in a day; display-name or portfolio issues can add a week. Coexistence depends on Meta eligibility—not a promise from any vendor.

Do I lose my WhatsApp Business App chats?

On Standard Cloud API registration, the same number usually leaves the mobile app. On Coexistence, the app can stay—read Meta’s current coexistence docs before you promise staff anything.

What is the first template I should submit?

A utility template tied to a real process you already run—order update, appointment confirmation, or payment receipt. Marketing templates should wait until utility is approved and quality rating is stable.

How does Samyoj pricing work with Meta?

Public plans: Launch trial, WhatsApp ₹899/month, Multi-channel ₹1,499/month, five teammates, 200 AI credits/month on paid plans. Meta conversation charges bill at actuals without Samyoj markup on published Meta rates—confirm on pricing.

Can I run Click-to-WhatsApp ads on day one?

Only if the number is active, billing works, agents reply fast, and you can track leads in one inbox. Slow replies waste ad spend more than slow SEO.

What is a Meta Tech Provider?

A partner track for software on WhatsApp Cloud API. Samyoj uses this wording today. BSP is a different track—verify in Meta’s directory before sales collateral says “official BSP.”

How do I collect opt-in for broadcasts?

Checkbox at checkout, keyword opt-in, or signed form at events—document the wording. Never buy phone lists.

What happens at the 250-recipient limit?

Many new portfolios start around 250 unique recipients per rolling 24 hours. Plan campaigns accordingly; scaling requires Meta’s quality and verification path.

Should I connect Instagram on day one?

Only if someone will answer Instagram daily. Otherwise master WhatsApp on the ₹899 plan first.

Where do I get official policy updates?

Meta WhatsApp documentation and WhatsApp Business Terms—blogs summarize; Meta decides.
