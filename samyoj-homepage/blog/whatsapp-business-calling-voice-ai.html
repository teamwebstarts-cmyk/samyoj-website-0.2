The Need for Hybrid Text + Voice Conversations

While 90%+ of initial customer queries are handled efficiently via WhatsApp text messages and automated AI chatbots, high-intent prospects often hit a complexity bottleneck. In 2026, forcing a customer to call an external 1800 number destroys thread continuity.

WhatsApp Business Calling API bridges this gap. Agents and AI bots can now escalate a text thread directly into an in-app WhatsApp voice call with a single tap.

1. Automated AI Qualification Call Handover

AI text bots pre-qualify leads and trigger an instant voice call to available human reps when lead score exceeds threshold.

2. High-Ticket B2B Deal Closing

Send proposal documents over WhatsApp text, then launch an instant voice call within the thread for live negotiation.
