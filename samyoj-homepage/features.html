01 WHATSAPP INBOX

Keep WhatsApp conversations ready for a reply.

Connect a business number and keep its inbox, contacts and campaigns in their own workspace. Assign chats, leave notes, search conversations and use ready replies. Its saved history stays if you disconnect the number.

  • Share images, documents, audio and video in a chat
  • Send free-form replies within 24 hours; use an approved template after that
  • See when a conversation started from an ad click
  • Create templates, submit them to Meta for review, and use buttons, lists or a call-permission card

02 AUTOMATION & FORMS

Build a simple path from message to next step.

Set WhatsApp welcome, keyword, away and business-hours replies. Use the visual flow builder for messages, buttons, delays and handoff, then test the flow or jump to a step from the inbox.

  • Collect details with WhatsApp Forms inside a chat
  • Use a separate keyword rule for Instagram comments
  • After someone replies to an Instagram comment message, continue in the Instagram inbox
Explore

03 WHATSAPP CAMPAIGNS

Plan WhatsApp campaigns with consent checks.

Choose an approved template and an audience from contacts, tags, a typed list or opted-in contacts. Fill in the template, then schedule or repeat a send.

  • Sending is blocked unless opt-in has a source and time, and the contact has not opted out
  • Retry failed sends and set a follow-up for people who do not reply
  • Review sent, delivered and read counts; pause, resume, reschedule or duplicate a campaign
Explore

04 AI CHAT ASSISTANT

Teach the chat assistant your business, then try it.

Set its personality and business details, add answers and FAQs, and test it before turning it on. The assistant can reply in the language a customer uses, including Hinglish.

  • Turn AI off for one conversation and reply yourself
  • Choose AI or human-only handling for a contact
  • Chat and calling assistants are configured separately
Explore

05 WHATSAPP CALLING AI

Review the call, then pick up the conversation.

The separate calling assistant can answer WhatsApp calls. Choose a voice and preview it, set whether AI or a person handles a contact, then review the call summary in the chat.

  • Business-level off setting takes priority over contact settings
  • Call recents and summaries stay with the conversation
  • Live transfer from the AI call to a human is not available yet
Explore

06 INSTAGRAM DMS

Keep Instagram DMs in their own inbox.

Connect a professional Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page. Read and reply to direct conversations from the Instagram inbox, within Instagram’s 24-hour reply window.

  • Instagram conversations remain separate from WhatsApp
  • When someone replies to a comment message, continue the conversation in DMs
  • Instagram requires a connected professional account and the linked Page

07 INSTAGRAM COMMENTS

Handle comment replies with the right limits built in.

Review comments, hide or unhide them, and send one private reply per comment within Instagram’s seven-day window. A keyword rule can start that reply from a comment.

  • Comment keyword automation runs on Instagram comments only
  • After the person replies, continue in the Instagram inbox
  • Samyoj removes comment rows after two days; the original Instagram post and comment are unchanged

08 INSTAGRAM PUBLISHING

Draft, schedule and publish Instagram posts.

Prepare image, Reel or carousel posts in Samyoj. Save a draft, publish now, schedule it, cancel a scheduled post, and review scheduled or published posts.

  • Keep planned content and published posts in view
  • Stories editing is not included in the current publishing tools

09 FACEBOOK MESSENGER

Manage Page messages with Messenger’s reply rules.

Connect a Facebook Page to use its separate Messenger inbox. Assign or close a conversation and send text replies within Messenger’s 24-hour window.

  • Messenger stays separate from the WhatsApp inbox
  • Page posts and WhatsApp templates on Messenger are not included
  • Page access depends on the required Meta permissions and approval

10 CONTACTS & LEAD CAPTURE

Keep contact details and incoming leads useful.

Search and filter contacts, organise them with tags, and keep WhatsApp campaign consent tied to its source and time. Facebook Instant Form submissions can be saved as leads.

  • Build WhatsApp campaign audiences from contacts, tags or opted-in people
  • Keep each connected WhatsApp number’s contacts in its own workspace
  • Save Facebook form submissions in the Samyoj lead list

Review ad results in Samyoj. Edit them in Ads Manager.

  • Meta account access depends on required permissions and app approval

Frequently Asked Questions

What features does Samyoj offer for omnichannel communication and lead automation? +

Samyoj connects WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, and Meta Ads into one AI-powered workspace. You get a unified team inbox, 24/7 AI lead qualification, visual automated follow-up sequences, multi-channel broadcast campaigns, smart CRM pipeline sync, in-chat forms, and live analytics.

Why do businesses lose leads across WhatsApp and Instagram without Samyoj? +

Most businesses miss inquiries because messages are scattered across separate apps, responses take hours, and follow-ups are manual. Samyoj automates the entire journey from first message to qualification, CRM sync, and multi-step follow-ups so zero leads fall through the cracks.

How does AI Lead Qualification work in Samyoj? +

When an inquiry arrives from WhatsApp, Instagram, or an ad click, Samyoj's AI agent responds within 5 seconds, asks qualifying questions, captures requirements, and routes hot leads directly to sales agents or schedules consultations.

Can Samyoj automatically follow up with prospects who stop responding? +

Yes. Samyoj triggers automated multi-step follow-up sequences across WhatsApp or Instagram. These sequences deliver timely nudges, answer objections, and re-engage cold prospects, boosting conversion rates by up to 3X.

Can I manage contacts, lead stages, and support tickets in Samyoj? +

Yes. Samyoj integrates contacts, tags, pipeline stages, and support tickets directly with the conversation history across all channels, giving your team full context without switching tools.

How does Samyoj integrate with Meta Ads (Click-to-WhatsApp & Instagram Direct)? +

Samyoj captures leads directly from Click-to-WhatsApp and Instagram Direct ads, triggering instant AI qualification flows with zero form drop-offs, cutting acquisition costs and maximizing ad ROI.

Is Samyoj built on official Meta APIs? +

Yes. Samyoj is built directly on official Meta Cloud APIs for WhatsApp Business, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger, ensuring 100% policy compliance, enterprise security, and 99.9% delivery reliability.
