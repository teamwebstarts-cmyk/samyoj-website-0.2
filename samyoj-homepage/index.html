Frequently Asked Questions

How does Samyoj solve the problem of lost leads?

+

Businesses lose up to 60% of potential customers because inquiries are scattered across WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, and Facebook, and manual follow-ups are too slow or forgotten. Samyoj unites all customer communication channels into one unified inbox, triggers instant AI responses in under 5 seconds, qualifies prospect intent, and automates multi-step follow-ups so zero leads fall through the cracks.

Which customer communication channels does Samyoj support?

+

Samyoj connects with official WhatsApp Business API, Instagram Direct Messages (DMs), Facebook Messenger, Meta Click-to-Chat Ads, and website live chat widgets. All messages arrive in a single omnichannel dashboard, allowing your team or AI agents to manage conversations seamlessly without tab-switching.

How does AI Lead Qualification work on Samyoj?

+

Instead of sales reps spending hours asking repetitive screening questions, Samyoj conversational AI automatically asks tailored qualifying questions (such as budget, property preferences, course requirements, or clinical symptoms), captures verified contact details, scores the lead, and immediately routes high-intent prospects to the appropriate counselor or sales rep in your CRM.

Can Samyoj automatically follow up with leads who stop replying?

+

Yes. When an interested prospect goes cold, Samyoj triggers automated multi-step follow-up sequences across WhatsApp or Instagram. These sequences deliver timely nudges, answer common objections, offer consultation booking links, or share limited-time promotions, converting up to 3X more cold inquiries into active sales.

Does Samyoj connect with my existing CRM and business workflows?

+

Yes. Samyoj integrates seamlessly with popular CRMs (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, LeadSquared), Google Sheets, Shopify, WooCommerce, and webhooks via Zapier and Make. Captured leads, conversation summaries, and custom tags sync instantly into your existing sales pipeline.

Is Samyoj an official Meta Business Solution Partner?

+

Yes, Samyoj is built directly on official Meta Cloud APIs for WhatsApp Business, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger. This ensures official policy compliance, enterprise-grade data security, and reliable message delivery through official Meta infrastructure.

Does Samyoj offer a free trial?

+

Yes! Samyoj offers a 14-day free trial with full access to omnichannel messaging, AI lead qualification, and automated workflows. No credit card is required to sign up. You can also book a live demo with our solution specialists for guided onboarding.
