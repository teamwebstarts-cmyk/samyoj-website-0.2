Frequently asked questions

Is Meta WhatsApp charge included in the plans?

+

No. Meta WhatsApp conversation charges are billed separately at actuals with zero markup from Samyoj. You only pay Meta’s official rates for templates you send.

Do you offer onboarding and support?

+

Yes. All plans include dedicated support and guided onboarding to help you connect WhatsApp Business API, verify your number, and launch your first campaign.

Can I change or upgrade my plan later?

+

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing anytime from your dashboard. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.

How does the AI & Voice billing work?

+

AI and voice are billed on a pay-as-you-go metered model based on actual token and minute usage. There are no hidden bundles — you only pay for what you consume.

Is there a setup or procurement fee?

+

No. Samyoj does not charge any WhatsApp Business API setup or procurement fee. You only pay the plan subscription plus Meta and AI usage at actuals.

How does annual billing save money?

+

Choosing annual billing gives you about 20% off the monthly plan price. You still get the same features — just lower platform cost billed yearly.
