Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan really free?

+

No — Launch is a 14-day free trial, not a forever-free plan. You get 1 WhatsApp number, 1 team member and 100 contacts to test the product. After 14 days, pick WhatsApp (₹799/month) or Multi-channel (₹1,499/month).

Are WhatsApp charges included in my plan?

+

No. Meta bills separately per delivered business-to-user message. For Indian (+91) recipients, the current standard list rates before GST are ₹0.8631 Marketing, ₹0.1150 Utility and ₹0.1150 Authentication per delivered template. Non-template Service replies cost ₹0.1150 after the first 1,000 delivered service messages per business phone number each month. Eligible Click-to-WhatsApp free-entry windows can make qualifying messages free for up to 7 days. Utility templates are chargeable even inside the 24-hour customer service window from 1 October 2026. An authentication-international rate of ₹2.4971 may apply to eligible businesses under Meta rules. Samyoj adds no markup. Rates depend on recipient market; see Meta's official pricing documentation.

What happens after my trial ends?

+

After your 14-day trial, you'll be prompted to choose WhatsApp (₹799/month) or Multi-channel (₹1,499/month). Your data is preserved.

Can I change or upgrade my plan later?

+

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly, quarterly and annual billing anytime from your dashboard. Changes apply from your next billing cycle.

What is included in AI?

+

Paid plans include 200 AI credits/month (one reply, voice use, or studio action = 1 credit). Launch trial has no included credits. Need more? ₹299/month adds 150 credits, or pay-as-you-go above your included amount (~₹2/credit for now). Track balance in the dashboard.

How do monthly, quarterly and annual billing work?

+

Monthly is billed each month at full price. Quarterly saves 5% (WhatsApp ₹759/mo billed ₹2,277 every 3 months; Multi-channel ₹1,424/mo billed ₹4,272 every 3 months). Annual saves 20% (WhatsApp ₹639/mo billed ₹7,668 yearly; Multi-channel ₹1,199/mo billed ₹14,388 yearly). You can switch billing cycles from your dashboard at any time.

Can I add more team members or WhatsApp numbers?

+

Launch includes 1 WhatsApp number and 1 team member. The WhatsApp plan includes 1 WhatsApp number; Multi-channel includes 3. Both paid plans include 5 team members and up to 10,000 contacts. WhatsApp includes 5 workflows; Multi-channel includes 10 workflows. Extra numbers and AI packs can be added later.

Is onboarding included?

+

Both plans include onboarding guides to connect WhatsApp Business API. Email support is included. Talk to us if you need a guided setup.

Do I need a WhatsApp Business number?

+

Yes. You'll need a phone number to connect to the official WhatsApp Business API. Our onboarding guides walk you through number verification and Meta Business Manager setup step by step.
