Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Samyoj (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our customer-messaging and marketing platform and related services.

Information we collect

We collect the account and business details you provide when you register or update your workspace (name, business name, email address, phone number, password), the billing details needed for plans, subscriptions and GST invoices (billing name and address, GSTIN where provided, plan and subscription status), the names and email addresses of team members you invite, your communications with us (including messages to our in-app support assistant), and technical information such as IP address, device, browser, log and usage data.

When our customers use Samyoj to communicate with their own customers and leads, we also process that information on their behalf: contact details (name, phone number, email address, tags, notes and other details the business adds), message content and files exchanged over WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram Direct and the business’s connected Gmail, form submissions received in chats and from the business’s Meta lead forms, call transcripts and post-call summaries for calls handled through the platform, consent and opt-out records, content briefs and drafts created with the AI assistant, and contact lists the business imports or receives through its connected WhatsApp numbers, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. Our customers determine the information processed about their contacts and are responsible for obtaining the required permissions.

How we use information

We use information to provide, secure and improve the Service; operate shared inboxes, campaigns, automations, forms, pipelines and reporting; configure and run AI chat and calling bots on the knowledge the business supplies; deliver transactional emails such as email verification and password reset; manage accounts, subscriptions and entitlements; issue invoices; prevent misuse; send service-related communications; meet legal obligations; and, where permitted, share relevant product updates. We do not sell personal information and we do not use customer content to advertise.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect requests against tampering, and stores your interface preferences (theme, text size, font) in your browser’s local storage. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Our web application loads interface fonts from Google’s font servers, which receive your IP address when the fonts are downloaded.

Sharing and service providers

We may share information with the providers that help us operate the Service: Meta Platforms, to connect a business to the WhatsApp Business Platform through Meta Embedded Signup and to act on its behalf for Facebook Pages, Messenger, Instagram threads and comments, and Meta ad insights; Google, for “Sign in with Google” and for a business’s Gmail connection, where only permission to send email from the connected mailbox is requested and the mailbox is not read; Sarvam AI, for AI chat replies, speech recognition and speech synthesis; Microsoft Azure, for text embeddings used to search business knowledge; GMI Cloud, for image generation in the AI Studio; Razorpay, which processes payments on its own checkout and receives card, UPI and netbanking details that we do not receive; the email service used for transactional email; and our hosting and object-storage providers.

Where a business connects Samyoj to tools of its choice — such as API keys or webhook endpoints — information flows to those destinations under that business’s control. We may also share information with professional advisers and authorities where legally required. We require service providers to handle information only for authorised purposes and with appropriate safeguards, and information may be processed in India and in other countries where these providers operate.

WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and customer data

Samyoj operates on Meta’s business platforms (the WhatsApp Business Platform, Facebook Pages, Messenger and Instagram). Use of these channels is also subject to Meta’s applicable terms and policies. Customers remain responsible for their communications, consent, message content and contact data.

We record the consent signals a business captures, recognise opt-out replies (such as STOP) automatically, and hold back campaign sends unless a contact has opted in and has not opted out. Meta charges WhatsApp message fees to the business’s own account; our plan price is for the software. We process customer data on the business’s documented instructions, except where law requires otherwise.

Google Gmail and Google Workspace data

When an organization owner or admin connects a Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox, Samyoj requests openid and email to identify the selected account, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send to send email. Samyoj does not read, list, search, sync, modify, or delete inbox messages, labels, or settings.

Samyoj stores the mailbox address and encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens. For each send, Google’s Gmail API receives the recipient, subject, message body, and any selected attachment for delivery. Samyoj stores the recipient, subject, message body, Gmail message ID, send status, and timestamps so workspace users can view sent history. Emails may be sent only to contacts with recorded email consent. Pending attachments are stored privately, deleted after the send attempt, and expire after 24 hours.

Disconnecting Gmail clears Samyoj’s stored tokens but does not erase existing sent history or revoke the Google Account grant. Users can also remove Samyoj from their Google Account security settings. We retain the mailbox address and sent history while the workspace is active and for up to 30 days after account closure for export before deletion, except where longer retention is required by law or for security. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train or improve AI/ML models. Human access is limited to a specific user-approved support request, a necessary security investigation, or a legal requirement.

Our use of information received from Google Workspace scopes adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements in the Google Workspace User Data and Developer Policy.

AI features

The AI chat bot and the AI calling bot answer using the business details, answers, FAQs and files the business supplies through Train and related settings. For WhatsApp calls the business routes to the calling bot, we convert speech to text, generate a reply and speak it back; Samyoj does not record call audio, but a text transcript and a post-call summary are saved to the conversation thread. Conversation content required to generate a reply or a summary is sent to the AI providers listed above. Businesses can turn a bot off for all chats or a single chat, allow or refuse AI on a contact’s calls, and test a bot before it goes live.

Billing and payments

Paid plans are billed in Indian rupees. Payments and payment instruments (card, UPI and netbanking details) are handled on Razorpay’s checkout, and we receive payment status and subscription identifiers together with the billing details needed to issue GST invoices and credit notes. Where a business provides a GSTIN or billing address, we use it to prepare tax documents.

Retention and security

Message history is kept for the retention period set in the workspace, and older messages are deleted automatically (one year by default). Instagram comments are retained only briefly (by default around seven days) and are then removed automatically. Accounts, consent records, AI usage records, invoices, credit notes and audit logs are kept for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including legal, accounting and security requirements.

A workspace owner can export a machine-readable copy of its workspace data from Settings and can request deletion; after a deletion request is confirmed, workspace data is purged within a defined window, except information we must keep by law or for legitimate purposes such as tax records and security. We use reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect information — including hashed passwords, encrypted access tokens for connected accounts, signed-in-only access controls and audit trails — but no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law. Marketing communications may be opted out of at any time. Workspace owners can export workspace data and request deletion from Settings. If you are a customer of a business that uses Samyoj and want to access, correct or delete information that business holds about you, please contact that business directly; you can also stop its campaign messages by replying STOP.

To make a request to us, please contact our team through the Contact page.

Children

Samyoj is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted here with a revised date. For privacy questions or requests, contact Samyoj through our Contact page.
